About Me

I am a passionate technologist and leader, currently pursuing my MBA at Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management. My educational journey—ranging from a Bachelor's in Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison to a Master's in Information Systems with an AI concentration from Northwestern—has equipped me with a unique blend of deep technical expertise and strategic business insight.
I have a proven track record in designing and developing both mobile and web applications, leading cross-functional teams, and managing full product life cycles. Outside work, I enjoy recreation beach volleyball, snowboarding, and going on long runs. I am excited to leverage my technical expertise, leadership skills, and global perspective to drive meaningful change in innovative organizations.
