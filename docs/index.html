About Me

I am a product, analytics, and software leader with eight years of experience building digital-health products and using customer research, product data, and financial analysis to guide decisions and delivery. I am currently pursuing an MBA at Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management, focused on Marketing and Operations.

My background combines a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison with a Master of Science in Information Systems, AI concentration, from Northwestern University. That combination of engineering, analytics, and business training shapes how I approach product strategy, technical delivery, growth, pricing, customer retention, and investment decisions.

At Northwestern University, I lead product and platform work for NIH-funded digital-health programs. My work includes launching an iPad-based gaze-tracking platform for early-autism research, modernizing engineering delivery through CI/CD automation, building iOS applications for clinical-research workflows, and creating data products that help business and research stakeholders make decisions.

I am Director of Operations for the Kellogg Evening & Weekend Tech Club, where I coordinate event operations, speaker logistics, and member engagement. Outside work and school, I volunteer with Northwestern's Pain & Palliative Medicine Department and PAWS Chicago. I enjoy pickleball, snowboarding, strength training, dinner clubs, and following financial markets.
